Rent increase calculator

Work out the new rent after an escalation, what it adds up to over the lease, and when you must give notice before a fixed-term lease ends.

🏠 For SA landlords✅ Free🔒 Runs in your browser

How much can a landlord increase rent in South Africa?

There's no legal cap on rent increases for private leases. The increase must follow the lease: a fixed-term lease usually has an escalation clause (often 6% to 10% a year), and the rent can't be raised during the term unless the lease allows it. At renewal the parties can agree a new rent, and the Rental Housing Act prohibits unfair practices, so increases should be reasonable and in writing.

Notice before a fixed-term lease ends (Consumer Protection Act)

Where the CPA applies (a landlord in the business of letting and a tenant who is a natural person), section 14 says the landlord must tell the tenant 40 to 80 business days before the fixed term ends that it's ending, plus any material changes such as the new rent. If nothing is signed, the lease continues month to month on the same terms.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a maximum rent increase in South Africa?

No law caps private rent increases. The lease's escalation clause applies during the term; at renewal the new rent is agreed, and it should be reasonable.

How much notice must a landlord give for a rent increase?

Under the CPA, 40 to 80 business days before a fixed-term lease ends, with the new terms. During the term, only as the lease allows.
