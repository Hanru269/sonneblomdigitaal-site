Frequently asked questions

What are the tax brackets for 2026/27?

Seven brackets from 18% to 45%: 18% up to R245,100, then 26%, 31%, 36%, 39%, 41% and 45% above R1,878,600. The full table is above.

Did the tax brackets change in Budget 2026?

Yes. In Budget 2026 the brackets, rebates and medical credits were adjusted for inflation, after two years without an adjustment.

What is the 2027 tax year?

SARS names the tax year after the February it ends in. The 2027 year of assessment is 1 March 2026 to 28 February 2027, also written as 2026/27.
