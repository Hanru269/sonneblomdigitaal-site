Questions

Do I need to be on camera?

No. Your character does everything. You pick trends, generate, and post.

What tools do I need and what do they cost?

Higgsfield (from about $15/month) or pay-as-you-go fal.ai (cents per second of video). Stills are close to free. The kit has a full cost table, so you'll know the price of every reel before you make it.

Is this allowed on Instagram?

Yes, if you play by the rules: turn on Meta's AI label, don't fake expertise (no AI doctors), and keep it Instagram-safe. The checklist covers it.

How do I get the files?

Gumroad emails you the download right after payment. The Kit is a PDF + Notion-style markdown. PRO adds the Vault PDF and two spreadsheets (CSV).
