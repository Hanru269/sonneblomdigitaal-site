Privacy

This website does not use cookies, run trackers or collect personal information. It only shows our products and links to the shops where you buy them.

Buying: orders are placed on Etsy or Gumroad. Their privacy policies cover your account, payment and delivery details. We receive only what those shops pass on to fulfil your order (such as a delivery address for printed gifts) and use it for nothing else.

Newsletter: handled by Gumroad. You can unsubscribe from any email.

Social media: our Facebook Page and its posting tool publish our own posts only. We don't collect data about people who view or interact with them.

YouTube (AI Bot Race uploader): our own server uses the YouTube Data API only to upload, schedule and describe videos on our own channel and to read its public statistics. It does not access, store or share anyone else's YouTube data. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke access at any time on the Google security settings page.

Instagram: our posting tools publish reels and reply to comments on our own accounts only.

Data deletion: to ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted, send us a message through Etsy or our Facebook Page.
